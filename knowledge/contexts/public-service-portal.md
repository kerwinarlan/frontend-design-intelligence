# Context Guide: Public Information & Service Portals

Public service portals provide information, public records, transit schedules, or community resources to the general public.

## Core Design Principles

1. **Information Architecture First**: Group information logically by task rather than organizational hierarchy.
2. **High Bandwidth Efficiency**: Keep initial bundle sizes small for users on mobile cellular data.
3. **Clear Status Indicators**: Use explicit badges (Open / Closed / Processing / Under Maintenance) with semantic colors.
4. **Search Prominence**: Provide instant search filtering across public documents and service forms.

## Inappropriate Patterns
* Dark SaaS aesthetic forced onto a public school or library directory.
* Autoplay video loops consuming mobile user data.
* Complex hover gestures that do not work on touchscreens.
