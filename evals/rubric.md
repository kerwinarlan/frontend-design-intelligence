# Visual Quality & Portfolio Readiness Evaluation Rubric

This 14-dimension rubric provides a quantitative (0-10) and qualitative evaluation framework for assessing web applications and portfolio projects.

## The 14 Rubric Dimensions

| # | Dimension | Score (0-10) | Evaluation Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Visual Hierarchy** | /10 | Clear scanning path; primary focal point stands out immediately. |
| 2 | **Typography** | /10 | Modular type scale, appropriate line height, tight tracking on headers, zero measure overflow (>75 chars). |
| 3 | **Layout & Composition** | /10 | 4px/8px grid discipline, balanced whitespace, intentional asymmetry, clean container alignment. |
| 4 | **Spacing & Rhythm** | /10 | Consistent padding/margins (`p-2`, `p-4`, `p-6`, `p-8`); proximity principle applied correctly. |
| 5 | **Color Discipline** | /10 | Restrained palette (max 2-3 hues); high contrast text; WCAG AA contrast compliance. |
| 6 | **Interaction Quality** | /10 | All 5 interaction states present; custom focus rings; immediate tactile feedback. |
| 7 | **Motion Quality** | /10 | Purposeful spring physics; GPU-accelerated transforms; zero layout thrashing; reduced-motion support. |
| 8 | **Responsiveness** | /10 | Fluid scaling across `sm`, `md`, `lg`, `xl`; touch targets >= 44x44px; container queries where appropriate. |
| 9 | **Accessibility** | /10 | Semantic HTML elements; landmark tags; ARIA attributes; keyboard navigation flow. |
| 10 | **Content Clarity** | /10 | Direct, specific technical copy; no generic AI marketing fluff or buzzwords. |
| 11 | **Technical Storytelling** | /10 | Stack badges visible; architecture diagrams; measurable performance metrics; live demo. |
| 12 | **Originality** | /10 | Avoids cookie-cutter AI templates; distinct visual identity and personality. |
| 13 | **Perceived Craft** | /10 | Crisp 1px borders, subtle surface elevations, meticulous detail alignment. |
| 14 | **Portfolio Readiness** | /10 | Confidently presentable to recruiters, founders, investors, and senior engineers. |

---

## Scoring Tiers
* **85 - 100 (Showpiece Quality)**: Exceptional craft, sophisticated motion, flawless typography, strong technical storytelling.
* **70 - 84 (Portfolio Ready)**: Clean, professional, accessible, well-structured, ready for hiring review.
* **50 - 69 (Visually Mediocre)**: Functional but generic, inconsistent spacing, unrefined motion, lacking technical narrative.
* **< 50 (AI Slop / Unrefined)**: Heavy purple gradients, glowing borders, unreadable contrast, zero keyboard access, broken layout.

---

## Important Evaluation Rule
**MORE ANIMATION DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY INCREASE SCORES.** Gratuitous or distracting motion that slows down task completion REDUCES the Motion Quality and Portfolio Readiness scores.
